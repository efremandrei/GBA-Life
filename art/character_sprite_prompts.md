# Character sprite prompts

These three transparent four-direction sprite sheets were created with the built-in image generation tool, using `app/src/main/assets/player_avatar_sprite_sheet.png` as a style and layout reference. The supplied Andrei sprite sheet was kept unchanged.

Common prompt constraints for each asset: a different original person, crisp 16-bit handheld-era pixel art with hard pixel edges and dark outlines; same size and proportions as the reference; four separated full-body views in a 2×2 sheet (front upper left, back upper right, left lower left, right lower right); consistent outfit and backpack across directions; transparent background; no labels, interface, logos, shadows, extra people, or objects.

- **Maya** (`avatar_maya_sprite_sheet.png`): friendly adult woman with medium olive skin and long dark curly hair tied back; teal jacket, dark blue trousers, brown shoes, small coral backpack.
- **Amir** (`avatar_amir_sprite_sheet.png`): friendly young adult man with medium brown skin and short black curly hair; clean-shaven; mustard yellow jacket over dark shirt, navy trousers, brown shoes, small forest-green backpack.
- **Dana** (`avatar_dana_sprite_sheet.png`): friendly young adult woman with light warm skin and short auburn bob; plum purple jacket over pale shirt, dark trousers, brown walking boots, small blue backpack.
